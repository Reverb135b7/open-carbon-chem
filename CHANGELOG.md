# Changelog

All notable changes to OpenCarbonChem are recorded here. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and the project uses
[semantic versioning](https://semver.org/).

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-09

First public beta. All eleven milestones of the project handover are implemented
and covered by the test suite (239 tests).

### Added

- **Calculation engine** — dimensionally strict `E = AD × EF` with typed failures
  (`IncompatibleUnitError`, `FactorScopeError`, `MissingFactorError`,
  `AmbiguousFactorError`); a quantity is never combined with an incompatible
  factor, and a CO2 factor never stands in for a CH4 row.
- **GWP conversion** — IPCC AR6 (default), AR6 with the 2025 errata, and AR5
  sets, each with a source reference. Methane requires an explicit fossil or
  non-fossil basis because the sets differ.
- **Emission-factor registry** — full provenance per row, deterministic
  resolution by geography and vintage (newest factor at or before the reporting
  year), catalogue and substance listings for the UI, and published/illustrative
  separation so test data can never be mistaken for real factors.
- **Corporate accounting** — Scope 1 (stationary, mobile, fugitive, process),
  Scope 2 (location-based always; market-based only with contract factors), and
  the 15 Scope 3 categories, with organisation-level reconciliation and
  year-over-year comparison that reports missing data rather than inventing a
  change.
- **Product carbon footprint** — product and reference flow, cradle-to-gate and
  cradle-to-grave boundaries, eight life-cycle stages, mass/economic/physical
  allocation, and cross-checks against the corporate inventory to prevent double
  counting.
- **Analytics** — hotspot ranking by substance, category, scope, gas and
  activity; product carbon intensity; sensitivity tornado analysis; Monte Carlo
  uncertainty with seeded, reproducible runs.
- **Decarbonisation scenarios** — levers applied to a baseline inventory, with
  ordered results and per-lever contribution.
- **Streamlit dashboard** — 11 pages covering overview, activity data, factor
  database, Scope 1/2/3, PCF, hotspots, sensitivity, scenarios and reports.
- **Reporting and CLI** — Excel workbook with per-row provenance and
  reconciliation, PDF executive summary, and `opencarbonchem calculate` for
  headless runs. Both reports reconcile against the supplied rows and refuse to
  write if they do not.
- **Factor set** — 278 rows: 91 DESNZ factors each for 2024, 2025 and 2026, plus
  five IPCC 2006 carbonate factors. The CSV is generated by
  `tools/extract_factors.py` from DESNZ's machine-readable flat files against
  `tools/factor_spec.json`; every selector is asserted to match exactly one
  numeric cell.

### Changed

- Factor resolution now spans three vintages. UK grid electricity is 0.20705,
  0.17700 and 0.13096 kg CO2e/kWh for 2024, 2025 and 2026 respectively.
- Air freight is included for domestic, short-haul, long-haul and international
  routes, with and without radiative forcing.
- Reports that combine published CO2e factors (which embed AR5 GWPs) with
  gas-level rows converted using the configured GWP set now carry a "Mixed GWP
  basis" warning in the Excel summary and PDF limitations.
- The packaged CSV is written through `csv.writer`, so notes containing commas
  can no longer truncate or shift a row on read.

### Notes and limitations

- Factors are `published`, not `verified`: values are generated from and checked
  against DEFRA's own spreadsheets, but have not been independently reviewed.
- Coverage is UK 2024–2026. There are no non-UK grid factors, no 2027 set, and
  DEFRA's overseas-electricity sheet contains no values to transcribe.
- `uncertainty_pct` is blank where the source publishes no per-factor
  uncertainty; Monte Carlo intervals are sensitivity ranges, not published
  confidence intervals.
- The DEFRA, EPA and Ecoinvent connectors are honest stubs: they perform no live
  fetch and return nothing without a packaged file.
- Scenario results are totals per scenario; a per-category breakdown is not yet
  written.
- The project is not certified, verified or conformant with any standard. It
  implements calculation methodologies consistent with the GHG Protocol and
  ISO 14064/14067.
